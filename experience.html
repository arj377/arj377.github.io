Embedded Software Engineering Intern

Lotus

I worked on production embedded firmware for an assistive wearable, developing low-level C firmware and device drivers using Zephyr RTOS and the nRF Connect SDK. Much of my work involved translating hardware behavior into reliable firmware and debugging across the hardware/software boundary.

Power Management

I helped migrate the device's power-management firmware from the TI BQ25180 PMIC to the BQ25188. This required working directly with device registers and adapting the existing driver to differences in configuration and interrupt behavior between the two chips.

BQ25180 → BQ25188 4.5 V input regulation 1.5 A battery protection

IR Interference Detection

I developed firmware for detecting persistent infrared interference that could prevent the wearable from reliably decoding commands. The system tracks failed decodes over time, distinguishes interference from valid transmissions, and alerts the user when persistent IR noise is detected.

3 failed decodes 10-second window user fault alert
